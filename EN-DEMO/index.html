Frequently Asked Questions

Do you really teach fully in English?

Yes, at Rijschool Stripes you can choose an instructor who speaks English. Theory explanations and practical lessons are given entirely in English, so you understand everything right away.

Will the instructor also be understood during the CBR exam?

The CBR exam itself is conducted in Dutch. As part of your preparation we also teach you the key exam terms in Dutch, so you go into the exam well prepared.

A lesson costs the same as our regular rates, starting from €55 per lesson. Check out our packages for a full overview of the options.

Can I book a free trial lesson in English?

Yes! Your first trial lesson is free, even if you'd like to take it in English. Simply book through the website or contact us by phone.
